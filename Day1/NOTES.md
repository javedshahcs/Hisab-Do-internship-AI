## 1.AI (Artificial Intelligence):
Computers that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
Example: Siri or Google Assistant.
## 2.Machine Learning (ML):
A type of AI where computers learn from data instead of being explicitly programmed.
Example: Netflix recommending movies.
## 3.Deep Learning (DL):
A type of Machine Learning that uses neural networks to solve complex problems.
Example: Face recognition on smartphones.
## 4.Generative AI:
AI that creates new content like text, images, music, or code.
Example: ChatGPT writing answers or DALL·E creating images.
